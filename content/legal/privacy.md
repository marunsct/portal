We built the products so that your data stays with you. This website follows the same idea.

## What we collect on the website

**Nothing by default.** There are no analytics scripts, advertising pixels or third-party
trackers on this site. Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, requested
page, time, browser string) for 30 days for security and capacity planning.

**Fonts.** Unless the operator of this site has self-hosted them, pages load typefaces from
Google Fonts, which means your browser requests files from Google's servers. Google's
privacy policy applies to that request.

## What we collect when you start a demo

- **One cookie** (`ownstack_demo`) containing a signed, random session identifier, the
  product and the expiry time. It exists so the demo gateway can recognise your browser. It
  expires with the session (45 minutes by default) and is deleted when you end the session.
- **Optional form fields** (name, email, company) if the launch page asks for them. We use
  them to follow up on your evaluation and, if you asked for one, to send an evaluation
  licence. We keep them for 12 months after your last contact and delete them on request.
- **Demo activity.** Whatever you do inside a demo product is stored in that product's demo
  database until the nightly reset. Do not enter personal or confidential data.

## What we collect when you contact us

The support form stores what you type (name, email, company, subject, message), the time and
your IP address, so that we can answer and keep a record of the request. We keep support
tickets for three years.

## Who we share data with

Nobody, except service providers who process it on our behalf under contract (for example an
email provider delivering our replies) and authorities where the law requires it. We do not
sell or rent personal data.

## Where data is stored

On servers we operate in Japan. If you are in the EU or UK, your data is transferred to
Japan, which the European Commission and the UK government recognise as providing adequate
protection.

## Your rights

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to
its use. Write to hello@ownstack.example. We answer within a month.

## The products themselves

Software you install on your own infrastructure sends us nothing: no telemetry, no licence
check-ins, no crash reports. Your data in DevFlow, AgentCanvas and Matura is processed by you
on your servers; you are the controller and we never see it.

## Changes

The date at the bottom of this page is the current version. Material changes will be
announced on the changelog.
