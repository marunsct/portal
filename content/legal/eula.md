This is a summary of the standard agreement for reference. The signed order form and the
full agreement provided with your licence govern; where they differ from this page, they win.

## 1. Grant

Ownstack K.K. grants the licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to install and
use the licensed product on infrastructure the licensee controls, for the term, the number of
named users and the plan stated in the order form.

## 2. What you may do

- Install the product on any number of hosts and environments (development, staging,
  production, disaster recovery) within your organisation.
- Serve your own customers and tenants with it without limit; end users are not counted.
- Make copies for backup and for air-gapped installation.
- Modify configuration, templates, stack packs, prompts and plugins provided for that purpose.
- Review the source code where your plan includes source access, under the NDA.

## 3. What you may not do

- Redistribute, sublicense, resell or offer the product as a service to third parties
  without a reseller or service-provider agreement.
- Remove or circumvent licence verification.
- Reverse engineer the product except where the law permits it regardless of this clause.
- Use the product to build a competing product by copying its design.

## 4. Term and expiry

The licence runs for the term in the order form. On expiry the software continues to
function; access to new releases and to support ends until renewal. We will not disable
software you have deployed because a licence lapsed.

## 5. Updates and support

During the term you receive all releases of the licensed product and support at the level
of your plan. Response targets are published on the [pricing page](/pricing) and in the
order form.

## 6. Ownership

The product is licensed, not sold. We retain all rights in the software and documentation.
You own everything you create with it: repositories produced by DevFlow, workflows and
exported projects from AgentCanvas, your tenants' data in Matura.

## 7. Data

The product runs on your infrastructure and transmits no data to us. We process personal
data about you only as described in the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy). Where you need a
data-processing agreement for support access to your systems, we sign ours or yours.

## 8. Warranty

We warrant that the product will perform materially as documented for the term. Your
remedy is for us to fix the defect, provide a workaround, or refund the fee for the affected
period. Pre-release and evaluation licences are provided as they are.

## 9. Liability

Each party's liability is limited to the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim,
except for breach of confidentiality, infringement of the other party's intellectual
property, or liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 10. Escrow and source access

Where the order form includes source access, the licensee may use the source only to
review, audit and build the licensed product for its own use. Where it includes escrow, the
escrow agreement states the release conditions.

## 11. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days of notice. On
termination for your breach you must stop using the product; on termination for ours you may
continue for the remainder of the paid term.

## 12. Law

The agreement is governed by the laws of Japan; disputes go to the Tokyo District Court.
Enterprise agreements may specify another law and venue.
