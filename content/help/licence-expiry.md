---
title: What happens when my licence expires?
description: The software keeps running. Updates and support stop until renewal.
section: Licensing
order: 10
updated: 2026-10-19
---

We do not build kill switches into products our customers depend on. An expired licence:

- keeps every feature working,
- logs a warning at start-up and in the administration pages,
- stops your registry credentials from pulling new releases,
- pauses your support entitlement.

Renewing restores updates and support immediately. If a renewal is late for administrative reasons, write to sales and we will extend the registry access while it is processed.
