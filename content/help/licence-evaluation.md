---
title: How do I get an evaluation licence?
description: Ask on the support page; it is a full licence with a 30-day expiry.
section: Licensing
order: 10
updated: 2026-10-19
---

Use the [support form](/support?topic=licence) and tell us which product you want to evaluate and roughly how you plan to deploy it. You receive, usually within a business day:

- a licence file for the product,
- registry credentials to pull the images,
- the compose bundle for the current release.

Nothing in the product is disabled during an evaluation. When the 30 days end, the software keeps running; you lose access to new images and to support until you buy a licence or ask for an extension.
