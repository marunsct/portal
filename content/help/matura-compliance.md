title: Does Matura make my product SOC 2 or GDPR compliant?
description: No product can. Matura implements controls and produces evidence; the audit is yours.
section: Matura
order: 10
updated: 2026-10-19
---

Compliance is a property of an organisation, its policies and its evidence, not of a software package. What Matura does:

- implements many of the technical controls auditors look for (SSO, MFA, tenant isolation, audit logging, data export and erasure, retention, access reviews);
- scores an installation against control packs for SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, GDPR and the EU AI Act with `matura check`;
- exports evidence in OSCAL for your GRC tooling and can publish a trust page.

What remains yours: the policies, the organisational controls, the risk assessments and the audit itself. The documentation is careful about this distinction and so is our sales team.
