---
title: What can I do in the hosted demos?
description: Short sessions, local models, nightly reset.
section: Demos
order: 10
updated: 2026-10-19
---

- Sessions last 45 minutes by default. Start another when one ends.
- Up to five people per product at once. If all seats are taken you will see a waiting page.
- Every demo runs on a local model on our hardware. No hosted provider keys are configured.
- Outbound email, public internet access from tools and external MCP servers are disabled.
- Data is wiped every night. Export anything you want to keep.
- DevFlow runs take an hour or more on a local model; the demo has a completed run preloaded so you can see the result immediately.

If something in a demo looks broken, [tell us](/support?topic=demo) with the time and what you clicked.
