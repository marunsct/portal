The products are commercial and not open source. Three things are available:

1. **Source review under NDA** for Business and Enterprise customers, so your security team can audit what they run and, if you need to, build the images in your own pipeline.
2. **Escrow** for Enterprise agreements, released on the conditions in the contract.
3. **Open integration contracts**: Matura's SDKs, SQL and conformance tests, the AgentCanvas export format and DevFlow's handover layout are documented so that nothing you build is locked to us.

Ask on the [support page](/support?topic=licence) and we will send the NDA.
