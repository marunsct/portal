The source of every product is public.

| Product | Licence | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| DevFlow | Fair Core License (FCL-1.0-ALv2) | Free to run for your own internal use. A licence key unlocks the enterprise features (SSO, audit export, HA, evidence packs). You may not offer it as a competing product. Each release becomes Apache-2.0 two years after it ships. |
| AgentCanvas | Fair Core License (FCL-1.0-ALv2) | As above. |
| Matura | Apache-2.0 core, SDKs and conformance CLI | Use, modify and redistribute freely. The commercial enterprise pack (Japan control packs, enterprise-managed authorization, evidence connectors, support) is licensed separately. |

Why this model: your security team can audit everything you run before you sign, and the
two-year conversion means you can keep running and maintaining what you depend on even if
we stop. Enterprise agreements can add escrow for work that has not been released yet.

The integration contracts (Matura's SDKs, SQL and conformance tests, the AgentCanvas export
format and DevFlow's handover layout) are documented so that nothing you build is locked to us.
